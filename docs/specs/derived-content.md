# Derived content (Phase 3) — designed once, computed from data already held

**Status:** designed 2026-09-04 (Fable). Fire counts are computed by `scripts/corpus-20k/derived/*`
(Opus) once Phase 2 coverage exists; a seed firing on fewer than 40 compounds is discarded and the
count recorded. Every derivation below is a **report of what the evidence does and does not
establish**. None is guidance. If a seed can only be phrased as guidance it is dropped here, with
the reason.

Notation: `{name}` is the compound's display name; braces carry the compound's own values, verbatim
from the source field named in the computability column. A question renders only when every value
it carries is `present`. R2 suppression removes seeds 1, 2 and 6 absolutely, and any other seed
whose wording names the reader ("you", "yourself") is never generated for a suppressed compound.

## Hard guard, applied to every seed

Every body renders three parts at most: what was measured (with organism, N, duration, route as
recorded), what the source states, and what was not measured. No sentence has the reader as its
subject except the question of seed 2, whose answer is "which of the three facts is missing", and
seed 2 never renders for a suppressed compound. Words that never appear in derived text: take,
dose (as a verb), start, try, protocol, recommended, should, safe, effective, optimal.

## Output contract for the executor

`data/corpus-20k/derived/fire-counts.json`: per seed { computable: bool, fires: n, sample: [10 keys],
discarded: bool, reason }. `data/corpus-20k/derived/<seed>.ndjson`: per compound the values the
renderer needs, with sources and dates. The question strings are produced by the R7 derivation
rules (`docs/specs/question-derivation.md`), not by this stage.
