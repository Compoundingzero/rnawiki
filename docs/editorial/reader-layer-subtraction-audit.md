## Reusable publication rule

A visible section needs a sourced answer to a reader question. Before calling it unavailable, search beyond the current RNAWiki record: the original trial, its registry, regulator, official fact sheet, and relevant review. Preserve a neutral unresolved answer only after that search, and do not manufacture a generic card. A diagram needs exact groups, measure, period, numbers, and an accessible text equivalent. No diagram appears for an unreviewed or purely mechanistic claim.
