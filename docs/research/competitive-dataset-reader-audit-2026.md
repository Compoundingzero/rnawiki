# Competitive dataset and reader audit, 2026

**Audit date:** 2026-08-31

**Mode:** expert desk review; no participant testing
**Scope:** the public product, provenance, update, reuse, and contribution surfaces of sixteen
named resources, using only first-party public documentation.

This audit asks two different questions of every resource:

1. What reader job does the product solve well?
2. Which source-bound facts, if any, can RNAWiki lawfully and reproducibly import?

A strong reader experience is not evidence authority. A primary database is not automatically
clear to a patient. “Import” below means a bounded, versioned field-level ingestion with the
upstream identifier, retrieval time, source-specific rights decision, and raw-artifact digest.
It never means copying a page or adopting its conclusion. Product features and terms can change;
rights must be rechecked at ingestion time.

## Strategic conclusion

- **Import:** versionable public or permissively licensed facts from FDA and Drugs@FDA, DailyMed,
  openFDA, PubChem, ClinicalTrials.gov, and narrowly permitted NCBI/PubMed metadata. Keep the exact
  upstream database, record identifier, version or update date, provenance, and licence decision.
- **Project:** only deterministic views over imported facts—for example counts, timelines,
  freshness, source agreement, and chemical-property displays. A projection must link back to its
  inputs and must not invent a medical conclusion.
- **Link:** all sixteen resources where they answer a reader’s next question. Commercial and
  consumer-editorial resources are destinations, not authority badges.
- **Exclude:** proprietary prose, tables, images, calculators, formularies, user reviews,
  ConsumerLab results, unlicensed abstracts or full text, and any record whose reuse rights cannot
  be established.
- **Defer:** ClinicalTrials.gov posted-results interpretation, structured NCCIH fact-sheet
  ingestion, broad literature extraction, and commercial licensed integrations. Each needs its own
  reviewed pipeline and acceptance gate.

> RNAWiki organizes source-bound medicine facts, uncertainty, disagreement, and change history. It
> is not a clinician, regulator, approved label, literature index, chemical registry, trial
> registry, supplement-testing laboratory, symptom checker, or personalized interaction or dosing
> tool.

## Product lessons that transfer without copying

1. Put the everyday question and answer boundary first; preserve exact evidence, identifiers, and
   history in deeper layers.
2. Separate regulatory fact, submitted record, secondary explanation, machine normalization, and
   community experience. They are not interchangeable evidence classes.
3. Show source identity, version/update date, and correction history beside the field they govern.
4. Give professional readers fast retrieval without forcing database terminology onto the first
   screen.
5. Use tools only when their inputs, limitations, validation, and clinical boundary can be stated
   honestly. An outbound link is preferable to an unvalidated imitation.
6. Make missing, conflicting, not-comparable, unavailable, and confirmed-stale states visibly
   different. None authorizes automatic medical rewriting.
