GtoPdb - Financial Sustainability

Commercial organisations that use GtoPdb pay access-fees which support the maintenance, curation and sustainability of the resource.

Our current commercial access fees are:

Organisation Size* Access Fees (per annum)**
Large (250+ employees)£2,000 GBP
Medium (50-249 employees)£1,000 GBP
Small (<50 employees)£500 GBP
* Employee numbers given are an approximate guide. See Definition of an SME (www.gov.uk)
** A single annual payment covers all users within a company.

To arrange payment, please get in touch with the Guide to Pharmacology curatorial team at: gro.ygolocamrahpotediug@seiriuqne, using the subject 'GtoPdb Access', and provide us with your organsation's name and main contact person. We will then get in touch to arrange the next steps.

Invoices will be arranged and managed by Edinburgh Innovations, the University of Edinburgh's commercialisation service.
