Reach is read from `data/revamp/presence-applicable.ndjson` against `data/revamp/thresholds.json`, and a pair takes the strongest reach of its two pages.

- **indexable** — the page's tier holds a derived threshold and the page meets it. Tier 1's threshold is 7 present fields over applicable; tiers 2 and 3 hold no threshold, so no page of theirs is indexable.
- **band** — the residual band. A tier with no derived threshold contributes the set at or above its own `ruleAnswerAfterStubFloor`: the count the unchanged rule proposes and the all-pairs line check then rejects. That is tier 2 at 8 present fields, the set `thresholds.json` measures at positional 0.138846 (clearing the 0.20 line) and lexical 0.412005 (over the 0.353 line). Tier 1 holds a threshold, so its rule-answer set is the indexable set and it contributes no band; tier 3 has no rule answer at all.
- **rest** — every other page.
