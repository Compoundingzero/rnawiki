### [2026-09-06] Phase 3 Step 3.4 — second-opinion adjudication prepared for the lead

Command: `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/identity_adjudicate.py`

Outputs:

The script joins `identity/remainder.csv` and `identity/second-opinion.csv` row for row (order and pair
identity asserted), then classifies each second verdict by whether a rule already written in
`docs/specs/identity-resolution.md` licenses applying it on the evidence that second opinion cites.
Every cited identifier is confirmed against the corpus's own record in
`identity/spine-attached.parquet` before it can license anything.

Result over 680 rows (644 distinct pairs): LICENSED-MERGE 6, LICENSED-FORM_OF 7,
LICENSED-SEPARATE 453, NOT-LICENSED 214. The 214 divide into 150 rows that record a merge identity
pass 2/3 already put in force — confirmed for all 150 against `presence-applicable.ndjson`, the
merged-away key absent and the survivor live — and **64 pairs a person still has to decide**: 23
touching an indexable page, 7 the residual band, 34 the rest. At 60 seconds a pair that is **64
minutes, under two hours**, with no further rule-derived reduction needed.

Nothing was applied. Recommended defaults in the draft come only from the spec: a merge the rules do
not license defaults to separate (§1), a form_of defaults to the existing state.

Four points for the lead:
1. Eight merge rows at high confidence rest on the sentence "one page holds no structure of its own"
   while the spine records a full InChIKey on both pages with different skeletons. All eight are held.
2. §2 reserves a K1/K2 disagreement for a person, so all 29 non-separate rows whose first-pass reason
   is that hold stay held whatever the second verdict — including 15 `form_of` verdicts that cite a
   GSRS ester relation (clobetasol propionate, cortisone acetate, megestrol acetate and so on).
3. `HOLD:existing:anhydrous-citric-acid` and `HOLD:existing:potassium-citrate` sit on both lists;
   apply the licensed merge before their held pair is reopened.
4. Eight apply-list rows come from pairs the first pass held as a whole chain because an exception
   class separates another link; applying them needs the transitive closure checked first.

The residual band had no recorded definition in `data/revamp`, so the script derives one from
`thresholds.json` and states it in the draft: a tier with no derived threshold contributes the pages
at or above its own `ruleAnswerAfterStubFloor` (tier 2 at 8 present fields, measured positional
0.138846 against the 0.20 line and lexical 0.412005 against the 0.353 line). Tier 1 holds a threshold
of 7, so its rule-answer set is the indexable set; tier 3 has no rule answer.
