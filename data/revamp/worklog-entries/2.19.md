### [2026-09-06] Phase 2 Step 2.19 — source integration, after-census, threshold re-derivation

Lead integration of the Phase 2 sources onto the corpus field records, then the after-census, the
delta and the re-derived thresholds. Nothing in this step fetches anything and DDInter is never
read.

**Commands and outputs**

- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/integrate_sources.py`
  → `data/revamp/fields-v2/{longevity,clinical,development}/batch-*.ndjson` (28,832 pages,
  1,000 per batch, 31 batches, 592 MB), `data/revamp/fields-v2/integration-summary.json`,
  and the licence-gated `data/revamp/fields-v2-gated/` (1,309 pages carrying `pgx`).
  Precedence rules: `docs/specs/field-integration.md`.

  --out data/revamp/field-census-after.csv --out-json data/revamp/field-census-after.json`

- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/derive_threshold.py --fields-dir data/revamp/fields-v2
  --census data/revamp/field-census-after.csv --out data/revamp/thresholds-after.json`

  `data/revamp/presence-applicable-after.ndjson`,

- Two rules govern the merge and both are recorded in `docs/specs/field-integration.md`: the

  register finding ("not found in the HSA listing as of 2026-08-07", an Inxight
  `interactionFound: false` row, an ARTG status never retrieved) is written into the value for the

- **Tier 1 is the real gain.** The threshold rose from 7 to 12 because pages carry more, and the
  indexable set still grew, 993 → 1,061. The set holds both lines with room: positional all-pairs
  0.1602 against 0.20, lexical size-matched 0.3287 against 0.353.
- **Tier 2's threshold of 20 is a degenerate selection and must not be read as an indexable tier.**
  The bucket rule answers 17; the sets at 17, 18 and 19 fail the lexical line (0.3984 at 551 pages,
  0.3940 at 271, 0.3731 at 68), and the rule's "rise by the smallest whole step that clears both
  lines" then lands on 20, where the set is a single page and both medians are trivially 0. Tier 2
  is still not indexable. What did move is the distance to the line: the best qualifying set went
  from 0.4027 lexical before to 0.3731 after, against the 0.353 line.
- **Tier 3 still has no threshold.** Every candidate from 3 to 15 fails: positional all-pairs runs
  0.7105 at 19,815 pages down to 0.1229 at 5, and lexical size-matched never falls below 0.3728.
  The fields added information but the rendered text is still near-identical between Tier 3 pages;
  that is Phase 4's differentiation work, not a data-coverage problem.
- **The stub count changed for a real reason.** Tier 3 pages holding fewer than 3 *applicable*
  fields fell from 3,512 to 0, because `registry`, `identifiers`, `potency`, `publicationYears`,
  `cyp_profile`, `contraindications`, `boxedWarning`, `controlled`, `regulatory` and `patentStatus`
  are now carried on every page and were searched for on every page. That is a change in what was
  looked for, not in what was found: Tier 3's median *present* count moved 2 → 4.
- `pending_source` is now empty, so no field leaves the denominator. The only entries a structural
  rule still removes from both numerator and denominator are 135 `withdrawal` entries recorded
  `present` on pages whose `withdrawn` is false (132 before; the three new ones are the ChEMBL
  `drug_warning` fills).
