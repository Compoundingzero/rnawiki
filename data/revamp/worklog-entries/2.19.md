### [2026-09-06] Phase 2 Step 2.19 — source integration, after-census, threshold re-derivation

Lead integration of the Phase 2 sources onto the corpus field records, then the after-census, the
delta and the re-derived thresholds. Nothing in this step fetches anything and DDInter is never
read.

**Commands and outputs**

- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/integrate_sources.py`
  → `data/revamp/fields-v2/{longevity,clinical,development}/batch-*.ndjson` (28,832 pages,
  1,000 per batch, 31 batches, 567 MB), `data/revamp/fields-v2/integration-summary.json`,
  and the licence-gated `data/revamp/fields-v2-gated/` (1,309 pages carrying `pgx`).
  Precedence rules: `docs/specs/field-integration.md`.
- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/field_census.py --fields-dir data/revamp/fields-v2
  --out data/revamp/field-census-after.csv --out-json data/revamp/field-census-after.json`
- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/census_delta.py` → `data/revamp/field-census-delta.csv`
- `.venv-corpus/bin/python scripts/revamp/derive_threshold.py --fields-dir data/revamp/fields-v2
  --census data/revamp/field-census-after.csv --out data/revamp/thresholds-after.json`
  → `data/revamp/thresholds-after.json`, `data/revamp/thresholds-after.md`,
  `data/revamp/presence-applicable-after.ndjson`,
  `data/revamp/field-census-applicable-after.csv`. `--fields-dir` and `--out` are new flags; the
  selection rule, the lines, the basis, the fold construction and the stub-floor guard are
  unchanged, and `data/revamp/thresholds.json` and `thresholds.md` are untouched.

**Result**

- 17 of the 18 Phase 2 sources carry a `mapped.parquet`; MHRA/emc holds zero rows and the UK
  jurisdiction block reads `UK register not cleared for this run` with the reason. Every mapped key
  in every source is already a corpus page key, so the component and combination fan-out the
  mapping rules require was done at map time and is consumed here unchanged.
- Eight new fields: `cyp_profile`, `contraindications`, `boxedWarning`, `controlled`, `potency`,
  `publicationYears`, `registry`, `identifiers`. `patentStatus` leaves `pending_source`;
  `regulatory` is written onto DEVELOPMENT records, which did not carry it, because G2 requires a
  Singapore status on 100 % of pages.
- `universal_blank` is now empty. It held `patentStatus` alone before.
- Two rules govern the merge and both are recorded in `docs/specs/field-integration.md`: the
  incumbent corpus value is kept unless the new source is strictly more specific, and a negative
  register finding ("not found in the HSA listing as of 2026-08-07", an Inxight
  `interactionFound: false` row, an ARTG status never retrieved) is written into the value for the
  reader and never makes a field `present`.
