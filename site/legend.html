How to read RNAwiki

#Evidence stars

Stars rate human evidence for a compound overall — not for your particular goal, and not how well it will work for you. Animal-only evidence is capped at two stars and labelled.

  • ★★★★★ — consistent, replicated human trials
  • ★★★★☆ — good human evidence, some inconsistency
  • ★★★☆☆ — mixed or limited human trials
  • ★★☆☆☆ — early, small, or animal-only evidence
  • ★☆☆☆☆ — mechanistic or anecdotal only

#Regulatory badges

A badge says who has approved a molecule. It is not a statement about where you can buy it. A medicine can be approved and still be prescription-only.

  • 🟢 — FDA Approved
  • 🟡 — OTC Supplement
  • 🔵 — Prescription
  • 🟠 — Off-Label
  • 🔴 — Not Approved
  • ⚫ — Controlled

#Availability

Availability is shown separately from approval: over the counter, pharmacy medicine, prescription only, controlled, or not approved. The classification we show is Singapore's, because that is the one regulator we track in full — it is a good guide almost everywhere, but check your own country's rules before assuming. Where a compound is prescription-only we say so and do not give a dose.
