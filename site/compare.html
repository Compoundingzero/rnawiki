123 pairs of compounds that people actually weigh against each other, because they are used for the same goal. Each page puts the two side by side on human evidence, mechanism, side effects, interactions and roughly what each costs.

Only supplements and over-the-counter compounds are compared this way. I do not publish a "which works better" page that ranks a prescription or controlled medicine against a supplement — you cannot act on that comparison, and in Singapore advertising a prescription-only medicine to the public is prohibited.
