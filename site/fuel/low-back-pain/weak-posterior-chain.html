Fuel — Lower Back Pain

#Weak posterior chain / deconditioning

What to eat for this cause, how much of it, and why each target is the target. The day-by-day log needs JavaScript; everything below does not.

#Daily targets, and why

NutrientTargetWhy this one
omega-3 (EPA+DHA)2000mg (goal)EPA/DHA lower inflammatory eicosanoids
fibre30g (goal)feeds anti-inflammatory — Something that reduces inflammation. short-chain fatty acids
added sugar30g (limit)excess sugar is pro-inflammatory
protein130g (goal)~1.6–2.2 g/kg drives muscle protein synthesis — The process of building new protein, such as muscle.
energy2400kcal (goal)adequate energy for anabolism
magnesium400mg (goal)cofactor for muscle contraction and recovery

#Using this

Food is the slowest lever on this list and the one that holds. Hit the targets from meals first; a supplement is for the gap you cannot close that way, which is why the compounds sit on the protocol page rather than this one.
