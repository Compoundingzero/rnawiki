The master pathways

Almost every compound here works through one of these. Learning the pathway once explains a whole family of compounds at a time — this is the layer that turns a list into an understanding.

  • GPCR → cAMP (the cell's doorbell) — 14 compounds
  • Nuclear receptors (rewriting the cell's instructions) — 37 compounds
  • mTOR (the "build" switch) — 6 compounds
  • AMPK (the "low-fuel" switch) — 5 compounds
  • Nitric oxide → cGMP (open the pipes) — 6 compounds
  • Growth-factor signalling / PI3K–Akt (grow and survive) — 15 compounds
  • GABA vs Glutamate (the brain's brake and accelerator) — 13 compounds
  • Dopamine, noradrenaline & serotonin (drive and mood) — 25 compounds
  • Acetylcholine (memory and mind-muscle) — 7 compounds
  • The testosterone loop (HPTA) — 24 compounds
  • The stress axis (HPA / cortisol) — 10 compounds
  • Mitochondria (your cellular power plants) — 17 compounds
  • NAD⁺ and sirtuins (the longevity fuel) — 3 compounds
  • Inflammation (the NF-κB switch) — 14 compounds
  • Melanocortin (tanning, appetite, libido) — 4 compounds
  • Cleanup crews: senescence & autophagy — 7 compounds
